Who can use it

You must be 18 or over. Cirrus is about reducing and stopping nicotine use; it is not appropriate for minors, and the app will not let you continue setup if you tell us you are under 18.

What Cirrus is, and is not

Cirrus is a self-help tool: it tracks what you log, calculates a tapering plan from it, and gives you a coach to talk to. It is not medical treatment, and it does not replace a doctor, a pharmacist, or a quitline. Ember, the in-app coach, is a language model. It will not give you medical advice, dosing guidance, or a diagnosis, and you should not treat anything it says as any of those.

If you are in crisis, please contact your local emergency services. In the US and Canada you can call or text 988 at any time.

Posts are screened automatically before they appear and reviewed by a person when flagged. We aim to review reported content within 24 hours. We can remove content and suspend accounts that break these rules. You can report or block from any post.
