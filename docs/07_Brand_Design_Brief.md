## 2. BRAND FOUNDATION

**Mission:** help people take their actual last puff — with honest math, a coach in their pocket, and people who get it.
**Personality (5 traits):** Honest · Warm · Blunt · Alive · On-your-side.
**Enemy:** not the user, not even the vape — the *system*: addictive product design + predatory quit apps.
**Tagline (recommended):** **"Your last puff is closer than you think."**
Alternates: "The last one, for real." · "Quit like you mean it."
**Voice rules:** sentence case, contractions, zero clinical language, zero shame words (see Doc 4 lexicon). Marketing copy passes the same honest-stats rule as the app (Doc 2 §8).

## 3. VISUAL IDENTITY (official — consolidates "Midnight Ember")

- **Palette:** Void #0A0C10 · Slate #161A22 (borders #232A36) · **Volt #C8F542** (primary) · **Ember #FF8A00** (streaks/celebration) · Oxygen #6EE7FF (breathing/calm) · Relapse Red #FF5C5C (alerts, kind copy) · Text #FFFFFF / #9AA3B2.
- **Type:** Space Grotesk (700/500) for display + all numbers (tabular) · Inter (400/600) body. Never serif, anywhere.
- **Motion:** every tap reacts; counters roll (odometer); confetti = Ember+Volt particles at true milestones only; reduced-motion respected.
- **Photography/imagery:** none. This brand is UI, numbers, and Ember. No stock humans, no vape glamour shots ever.

## 5. EMBER — CHARACTER BIBLE 🔥

**Who:** the user's streak flame come to life. Not a pet, not a child — a small, cool-headed companion with best-friend energy. Duolingo-owl memorability, but calm and 18–30-coded, never kiddie.

**Form:** a compact teardrop flame with simple expressive eyes (no mouth needed — eyes + body language carry emotion). Body gradient Ember→Volt at the edges. Soft glow. Max 3 shape details so it reads at 24 px (chat avatar) and 200 px (empty states).

**Evolution states (must match Doc 3 §5 exactly):**
| Streak | State | Look |
|---|---|---|
| 1–2d | Spark | tiny wisp, big hopeful eyes |
| 3–6d | Flicker | small flame, steadier |
| 7–13d | Flame | confident, upright |
| 14–29d | Blaze | fuller body, Volt edge glow |
| 30d+ | Inferno | crowned flame, particle aura |

**Expression set (design all 6):** idle breathing loop · cheering (milestones) · steady-calm (panic mode — slows its flicker to the 4-7-8 breath rhythm, brilliant detail: *the mascot breathes with you*) · concerned-warm (slip day) · dimmed (repair-token day) · sleeping (quiet hours).
**Usage:** coach chat avatar · Home streak flame · empty states · onboarding C5 build animation · notification icon accent · TikTok watermark corner.
**Never:** Ember never scolds, never cries dramatically, never appears in ads glamorizing vapes, never speaks in third person about itself in a cutesy way.

## 6. PASTE-READY PROMPT — EMBER CHARACTER SHEET (Claude Design)

```
Using the same "Midnight Ember" design system (Void #0A0C10 background, Volt #C8F542,
Ember #FF8A00, Space Grotesk + Inter, no serifs), design a character sheet for
"Ember" — the mascot of quit-vaping app LastPuff. Ember is the user's streak flame
come to life: a compact teardrop flame with simple expressive eyes (no mouth), body
gradient Ember-orange core to Volt-lime edges, soft glow, max 3 shape details so it
reads at 24px and 200px. Personality: warm, calm best friend — cool and modern for
ages 18–30, never childish or cutesy.

Sheet contents, one artboard each:
1) Five evolution states side by side with labels: Spark (tiny wisp, hopeful eyes),
   Flicker, Flame (confident), Blaze (Volt edge glow), Inferno (crowned, particle aura).
2) Six expressions of the "Flame" state: idle · cheering with confetti · steady-calm
   breathing (slow, matches a 4-7-8 rhythm) · concerned-warm · dimmed (grey-orange) ·
   sleeping.
3) In-context mockups: 24px chat avatar in a message row · Home screen streak chip ·
   empty-state illustration ("No posts yet — say hi") · dark app icon tile with the
   flame glyph.
4) A "don't" row: no mouth, no limbs with hands, no photoreal fire, no purple, no
   kiddie proportions.
Dark backgrounds throughout. Number every artboard and end with a ✓ checklist.
```
